Our Story

A studio for living well

Rarama Living Studio is built around the idea that a meal is more than food — it is a moment of pause, a story shared, a craft respected. We draw on regional recipes and local ingredients to make dishes that feel familiar and a little surprising.

Our kitchen is small, our menu is seasonal, and every plate is meant to be enjoyed slowly, in good company.

Our Venue

An open garden for gathering

Set within a tropical garden village, our venue offers open spaces, traditional architecture, and a calm green setting — a beautiful backdrop for dinners, family meals, private gatherings, and special celebrations.

  • Garden venue in the heart of the city
  • Open pavilion and shaded terrace
  • Parking for cars and motorcycles
  • Family-friendly spaces and a peaceful pool
